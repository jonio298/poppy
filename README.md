# Charles Ross Memorial Slideshow

A full-screen photo slideshow created in loving memory of Charles “Charlie” “Dad” “Poppy” Ross.

## Two modes

**Play mode** is the clean slideshow.
- Tap or click **Begin slideshow** to start. The photos are timed evenly across the 22:24 playlist by default.
- **Phones:** swipe left/right to change photos, tap to show or hide the controls.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys to move, space to pause, **F** for full screen, **E** to switch to Edit mode.

**Edit mode** opens a side menu (a bottom sheet on phones). Switch back with **▶ Play** at the top of the menu.
- **Slides:** the full list of photos with thumbnails. Drag the **⠿** handle to reorder (or focus it and use the up/down arrow keys), tap a slide to preview it, **✎** to edit it, or the red **✕** to delete it (with Undo, and a Restore list for deleted slides).
- **Slide:** for the selected photo, change its date, add a caption, resize it, pick a photo style (thin white border, plain, polaroid, gold frame, film strip), pick its transition and preview it, move it earlier/later, reset it, or delete it.
- **Show:** the opening text, default transition and photo style, seconds per photo, Ken Burns motion, photo size, and the vintage film look.
- **Theme:** colour presets (or any background/accent colour) and font.

## Look

The default look is a soft crossfade between photos, a thin white border over a blurred copy of the photo as the background, a slow Ken Burns zoom and drift, and a subtle vintage film finish (grain, vignette, warm tone). Photos are shown as large as fit the screen and are never cropped, and the Ken Burns motion never pushes a photo past the screen edge.

## Saving

Edits save automatically in the viewer's own browser, so each person sees their own version and the original is never changed. Use **Download my version** (Slides tab) to back one up or move it to another device.

`photos/thumbs/` holds small previews used by the slide list.
