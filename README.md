# Charles Ross Memorial Slideshow

A full-screen photo slideshow created in loving memory of Charles “Charlie” “Dad” “Poppy” Ross.

## Two modes

**Play mode** is the clean slideshow.
- Tap or click **Begin slideshow** to start. It begins on the opening slide (“In Loving Memory”), which stays for 12 seconds (adjustable on the Show tab) before the photos start. The photos are timed evenly across the 22:24 playlist by default.
- **Controls:** outside full screen the play/pause controls live in the bottom bar, centered, with the "Now playing" song on the left and the time on the right (on a phone the song is on the first row and the controls on the second). In full screen the bar is replaced by a floating control bar that disappears and comes back when you move the mouse (or tap on a phone). In full screen, moving between photos with the arrow keys (or a swipe) only flashes the “12 / 114” counter in the corner; the controls come back when you move the mouse.
- **Phones:** swipe left/right to change photos.
- **Home:** the house button at the upper left of the slideshow goes back to the opening slide and stops, like Esc.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys to move, space to pause, **R** to restart, **F** for full screen, **E** to switch to Edit mode, **Esc** to leave full screen and pause; press **Esc** again (outside full screen) to go back to the opening page, where Edit mode is. Press **?** (or the keyboard button in the control bar) for the full list in a small card, instead of text permanently on screen.
- **Restart:** the ↻ button (or **R**) starts over from the first photo. When the show ends, a **Restart slideshow** button appears.
- **Auto-scroll:** the loop button (or the checkbox on the Show tab) keeps the show playing continuously, starting over after the last photo.

**Edit mode** opens a side menu (a bottom sheet on phones). Click **✎ Edit mode** again (or the lit **Edit** at the top of the menu) to close it. **▶ Play** at the top of the menu starts the show with the menu still open and becomes **❚❚ Pause**; the highlighted slide in the list follows along, kept near the middle (it holds still for a few seconds if you scroll the list yourself, and follows a folded song's bar if the slide is inside it).
- **Slides:** **＋ Add my own photos** (or drag picture files onto the page) adds pictures from your device. They are shrunk to a sensible size and kept in this browser, then behave like any other slide. Below that, the full list of photos with thumbnails; **hover a thumbnail with the mouse to see it at 400% (the full-size photo loads in as you hover)**. Drag any slide up or down to reorder (press and hold on a phone, or focus the **⠿** handle and use the up/down arrow keys), tap a slide to preview it, **✎** to edit it, or the red **✕** to delete it (with Undo, and a Restore list for deleted slides).
- **Slide:** (the first time you open this tab with nothing selected it jumps to slide 1, and the “1 / 114” counter shows in the corner) grouped into Text, How the photo looks, When it plays, Position, and “Use this slide’s choices on every slide” (copies its transition, style or filter to all slides). For the selected photo, change its date, add a caption, resize it, pick a photo style (thin white border, plain, polaroid, gold frame, film strip), pick a filter (black & white, sepia, vintage, warm, cool, vivid, faded, noir, soft glow) and its strength, pick its transition and preview it, set how many seconds it stays and its Ken Burns motion, apply its transition or style to all slides, move it earlier/later, reset it, or delete it.
- **Show:** (it ends with **Restore these settings to the defaults**, which resets everything on this tab but leaves your slides, text and theme alone) the opening text, default transition and photo style, seconds per photo, Ken Burns motion, photo size, and the vintage film look.
- **Theme:** colour presets (or any background/accent colour) and font.
- **Versions:** every **Save** keeps a dated, timed version here. Each card (and the original slideshow) has an **Opens on reload** switch: turn one on and that is what loads when you come back to the page; turn it off and the original opens instead (turning the original off picks your latest save). **Open** loads one into the editor, **Rename**, **Download** it (with your own photos inside) or **Delete** it. "On screen" marks the one you are editing right now.

## Songs and the timeline

The slideshow is timed to seven songs (What a Wonderful World, When I Fall in Love, Only You, Can't Help Falling in Love, In the Mood, Goodnight My Love, The Prayer; about 22:34 together). **This page does not play the songs.** Start them on your own device; the page shows which song each slide belongs to and times the slides to match.

- Edit mode → **Slides** tab (titled “Songs & slides”): a colored section for each song showing its time range, how many slides it has and how many seconds each slide gets.
- **Change a song's length:** click the ⏱ chip on its bar (or use the Show tab), type `3:05` or `185` and press Enter. Leave it empty to go back to the recording's length. The song's slides, the later songs' start times and the total all update; a dot on the chip marks a length you changed.
- **Caption text:** one line of handwriting under every photo (inside the Polaroid's white strip, or on a small white bar under the photo on every other frame). It is as wide as the photo and the type shrinks to fit. Edit it in the Slide tab under *Caption text*; the old separate multi-line caption box is gone.
- **Closing slide:** after the last photo the show ends on "Thank You / Poppy Loved All of Us", written out in handwriting one line after the other (12 seconds, like the opening). Change the words on the Show tab under *Closing slide* (one line per line of text), or empty the box to remove it.
- **Dragging a slide:** the list holds still while you drag, and a gold line shows exactly where the slide will land when you let go. Aim at the middle of a song's bar instead to drop it into that song (the bar glows green). Near the top or bottom edge the list scrolls, faster the closer you get.
- **Reorder the songs:** drag a song bar (by its ⠿ handle, or anywhere on the bar; press and hold on a phone) to move the whole song, with all its slides. While you drag, every song folds so only the bars show. With the handle focused, Up/Down arrows also move it. Numbers show the order the songs play in.
- **Fold a song:** click a song's colored bar to collapse its slides into it (click again to open). The arrow shows the state, **Collapse all songs / Expand all** are above the list, and the choice is remembered on that device. A folded song still shows its slide count and a marker if the current slide is inside it, and you can still drop a slide onto it.
- **Default sections:** the dancing, wedding, party, birthday-celebration and playful photos (36 of them, marked with `"song": 5` in `photos.json`) are in **In the Mood**; the other photos are shared across the remaining songs in order. Change any of it with Send to / drag, or edit the `song` values in `photos.json` to change the default for everyone.
- **Send to a song:** the ♪ button on any slide in the list, or **Send to…** at the top of the Slide tab, moves a slide into a song. You can also drag a slide into another song's section. You can also drag a slide and **drop it onto a song's bar**: while you drag, every song bar pulses green, and the one under your pointer glows brighter. The slide goes to the end of that song (even if the song is folded).
- **Now playing:** outside full screen, a bar under the slideshow shows the current song, its artist and the time within it (“Up next” on the opening slide, “Paused on” when paused).
- **Show** tab → **Songs:** turn the song timing on or off. Under **Adjust track length**, each song has − and + buttons (5 seconds a step, 30 with Shift) beside its time, which you can also type; a yellow time is one you changed. Drag a track by its ⠿ handle (or anywhere on the row; press and hold on a phone, or use Up/Down on the handle) to reorder the songs, and their slides move with them.
- **Slides per song:** each song holds only as many slides as fit its length at about 12 seconds each (a 3:10 song holds 16). The song bar shows "12 of 12 slides", and **Send to** or dragging a slide into a full song is refused with a message. The default arrangement fills every song to its length, so every slide runs roughly 12 seconds. Type a number in *Seconds per photo* on the Show tab if you would rather give every slide exactly the same length (the songs are then as long as their slides).
- **Slide titles** in the list, and the caption text under each photo, show the date plus a few words on who or where (the `short` field in `photos.json`). A date you type yourself replaces the whole label.
- **Jon's Version is the default show.** What opens when nobody has saved a version (and what Reset goes back to) is `jons-choice.json`: Jon's order and captions, 108 slides, divided across the songs so each song is filled exactly (the slides run 12.2 to 13 seconds). The **Jon's Version** switch under the Save bar in Edit mode turns it off to show the plain original (every photo, no captions, no reordering) and back on. To change Jon's Version, edit, press Download my version, and replace that file.
- **Default transition** is Random (never repeats) on a fresh load and after Reset.

## Look

By default the transitions are shuffled randomly (never the same one twice in a row; each slide can be assigned its own). Older photos (before 1990, adjustable on the Show tab) get a Polaroid look and newer ones rotate through thin white, rounded white, gold frame and cream mat borders. Every photo sits over a blurred copy of itself, a slow Ken Burns zoom and drift, and a subtle vintage film finish (grain, vignette, warm tone). Photos are shown as large as fit the screen and are never cropped, and the Ken Burns motion never pushes a photo past the screen edge.

## Saving

Press **Save** at the top of the Edit menu to keep a version. Versions, and any pictures added, are stored in that person's own browser (localStorage plus a second IndexedDB copy; a small cookie lists the saved versions so the page can tell if browser data was cleared), and the slideshow opens as the most recently saved or opened version every time, even after a reload. Unsaved changes are not kept on reload. **Reset** throws away unsaved changes and goes back to the last saved version (or, if nothing is unsaved, back to the original, which is only kept if you press Save). Ctrl+S also saves. Each person sees their own version, and the original is never changed. Use **Download my version** (Slides tab) to back one up or move it to another device.

`photos/thumbs/` holds small previews used by the slide list.
